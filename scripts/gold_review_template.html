How to label

  • One box per frame, around the staged object named above only - not other cars, barriers or curbs in the background.
  • Drag a tight box around the visible part. Redraw to correct.
  • Exclude shadows. Include the object's own stand or base plate.
  • Object cut off at the bottom or a side of the image: box the visible part and press T. A tall object running past the top edge is normal - no T.
  • Object not in the image (passed under the bumper, too far to see): N.
  • Only the object the vehicle is approaching counts. If it has already passed out of view and only similar objects further away are visible (e.g. other wheel stops across the parking lot): N.
  • Cannot decide: U - better than guessing.

How to label

  • One box per frame, around the staged object named above only - not other cars, barriers or curbs in the background.
  • Drag a tight box around the visible part. Redraw to correct.
  • Exclude shadows. Include the object's own stand or base plate.
  • Object partly outside the image: box the visible part and press T.
  • Object not in the image (passed under the bumper, too far to see): N.
  • Cannot decide: U - better than guessing.
